# SaaS Churn & Unit Economics Analysis

## What is this?

This project analyses a subscription/SaaS business and tries to answer a simple question:

**Why are customers leaving, and what does that mean for the business financially?**

It creates synthetic SaaS customer data and analyses:

- Revenue
- Churn
- Customer retention
- CAC
- LTV
- Payback period
- NRR
- GRR
- Customer cohorts
- Possible churn causes

## How the project works

```
Generate customer data
        ↓
Calculate SaaS metrics
        ↓
Analyse customer cohorts
        ↓
Find churn patterns
        ↓
Investigate possible causes
        ↓
Show results in dashboard
```

## Main files

- `generate_saas_data.py` — creates sample customer data
- `financial_model.py` — calculates business and financial metrics
- `cohort_analysis.py` — studies customer retention over time
- `root_cause_diagnosis.py` — looks for possible reasons for churn
- `app.py` — Streamlit dashboard
- `executive_deck/` — short leadership summary
- `tests/` — automated tests

## Important metrics

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**  
How much it costs to acquire one customer.

**LTV (Lifetime Value)**  
Estimated revenue/value generated by a customer during their relationship with the company.

**Churn**  
Customers who stop using or paying for the service.

**NRR (Net Revenue Retention)**  
Shows how revenue from existing customers changes after expansion, contraction and churn.

**GRR (Gross Revenue Retention)**  
Measures retained revenue without counting expansion.

**Payback Period**  
How long it takes to recover the cost of acquiring a customer.

**Cohort**  
A group of customers who started around the same time.
