# Executive Presentation: B2B SaaS Churn Diagnosis & Unit Economics Engine

**Author:** Ajit Pal Singh (Business Analyst)  
**Audience:** Executive Leadership, Head of Product, VP of Sales  

---

## Slide 1: Executive Summary & Health Check
- **Current Run-Rate:** Active ARR of **$2.4M+** with an aggregate **NRR of 106.4%**.
- **The Core Problem:** Topline revenue growth is masking a severe churn leak in the **Starter tier ($150/mo)**, where logo churn exceeds **35%** and CAC Payback stretches past **16 months**.
- **The Opportunity:** Aligning sales discount policies, restructuring onboarding milestones, and focusing acquisition on high-retention channels will boost NRR to **118%+** within 3 quarters.

## Slide 3: Root-Cause Diagnosis of Churn

1. **The "Discount Trap" (Sales Friction):**
   - Accounts acquired with >20% upfront discounts exhibit **1.8x higher churn rates** by Month 6.
   - Heavy discounting attracted low-intent buyers with low implementation commitment.

2. **The 90-Day Onboarding Cliff:**
   - **48% of all churn events** happen between Months 1 and 3.
   - Key indicator: Accounts opening <2 support tickets during the first 30 days had an **80% likelihood of churning by Month 4** (indicating zero adoption rather than smooth onboarding).

---

## Slide 4: Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

1. **Gate Discount Authorizations:**
   - Eliminate non-standard discounts (>10%) on Starter and Growth plans without VP approval.
   - Require annual prepaid commitments for any pricing concession.

2. **Automate Milestone-Driven Onboarding:**
   - Implement telemetry alerts when a newly onboarded account fails to configure their core integration within 7 days.
   - Trigger proactive Customer Success outreach at Day 14.

3. **Reallocate Growth Spend to Inbound & Referral:**
   - Shift 30% of paid outbound acquisition budget into product-led content and partner referral channels, which currently deliver **3.8x higher LTV:CAC**.

---

## Slide 5: Projected Financial Impact
- **Churn Reduction:** 20% relative reduction in Starter tier churn saves **$240k in annual ARR**.
- **Payback Compression:** Starter CAC payback compressed from 16.4 months to **10.8 months**.
- **Target ARR Impact:** Expansion in Enterprise + saved churn yields a projected **+$580k Net ARR expansion** over the next 12 months.
